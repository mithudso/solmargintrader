## Idea and objective

Map accumulation and distribution through Wyckoff phases while showing the latest confirmed event and pending requirements. The script also supplies a movable teaching schematic, separating an idealized visual reference from its automated structural assessment.

## How it works

- The algorithm advances through stopping action, range building, testing, strength/weakness and breakout phases. It combines weighted soft requirements with mandatory hard checks instead of relying solely on pivots.
- Historical and current schematics, range boxes and event labels appear when configured phase milestones are reached. A red invalidation marker records where a historical pattern failed.
- A confidence score determines optional entry labels, generally around phase C. Higher-timeframe searching can locate a more advanced regime than the current chart.

## Outputs and intended use

- Status and debug tables expose current phase, confirmed events, progression and requirement checks; side panels gray unconfirmed segments.
- The author intends instructional use and experienced-user confluence, while explicitly acknowledging false detections and missed textbook patterns.

## Parameters and stated settings

- Manual schematic disabled by default but still requires two initial corners. Historical patterns default to phase C and chart overlay to phase B.
- Higher-timeframe search enabled by default; entry strictness, phase triggers, alerts, panel size and display controls are adjustable.

## Failure modes and caveats

- Wyckoff narratives about institutional intent cannot be established from chart structure alone.
- Historical confirmed-event plotting must be distinguished from the information available when the event actually occurred.
