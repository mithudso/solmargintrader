## How it works

- Classify accumulation/distribution from volume-rate-of-change and price momentum, identify local fractal highs/lows, derive imbalance/low-liquidity zones and render volume-weighted price bands. Summarize flow, structure, smart-money state, liquidity and divergence in a HUD.

## Outputs and intended use

- Use boxes, heatmap, lines and alerts to inspect participation/structure transitions. The holographic language describes appearance, not an observed extra-dimensional dataset or direct visibility into a hidden order book.

## Parameters and stated settings

- Microstructure depth spans20-200 bars, heatmap resolution5-20 levels and the narrative mentions recent100-300 bars. Imbalance ratio, liquidity/accumulation thresholds, fractal layers, HUD and themes are configurable.

## Failure modes and caveats

- Candle price/volume cannot identify institutions or undisplayed orders by itself. Marketing assertions about hidden liquidity and precise flow are unsupported without the omitted data/algorithms.
