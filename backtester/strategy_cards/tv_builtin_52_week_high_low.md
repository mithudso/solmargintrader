# 52 Week High/Low

Version: 1.0.0 (2026-11-08). Delta: first source-reconciled card.

> **Spec-only — no strategy implementation or performance result is claimed.**
> This card records a signal or research method. An indicator alone is not an order rule.

## Mechanism and equations

Take max(high_source) and min(low_source) over the preceding 52 calendar weeks. Use timestamps, not a fixed bar count that silently changes with timeframe.

## Documented inputs and source details

TradingView catalog inputs: `high source`=close [close, high]; `low source`=close [close, low].
