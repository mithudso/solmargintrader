Oraclebot research journal

The journal records methods, assumptions, and limitations from Oraclebot's market and strategy research. It includes historical backtests and working papers. These studies do not establish future returns or authorize trades.

Working papers

What holds up on HYPE? Strategies, patterns and price levels (PDF) tests trading rules, price patterns and support and resistance levels on HYPE candles: two years of daily bars, 208 days of hourly bars and one week of minute bars. Over the two years no tested rule beat holding the coin, and the patterns and levels tested did not pass their controls. The paper reports historical backtests and has no prospective validation.

Do two candles predict a ZEC reversal? (PDF) examines whether the next ten-minute candle reverses direction after two moves in the same direction. It uses a single day of ZEC observations. The paper is exploratory and has no prospective validation.
