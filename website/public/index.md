# Oraclebot

Oraclebot is a research and paper-trading workspace. Explore market signals, test strategies against historical data, and save paper configurations for Coinbase spot or Jupiter leveraged markets.

The oracle can withhold a signal when evidence is stale or missing. Backtests depend on their data, assumptions, and execution costs. Historical or simulated results do not predict future performance.

[Read the public research journal](https://oraclebot.io/research/).
