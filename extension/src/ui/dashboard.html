    Grid experimental
    
            
    The Jupiter ladder grid. It lost to buy-and-hold walk-forward on 9 of 9 coins; it stays here for research and dry runs.
    
          
    
        
    
        
    
        
    
          Educational tool, not financial advice. Grid trading loses money in a trending market, and any
          leveraged position can be liquidated. Dry-run mode is the default for a reason.
