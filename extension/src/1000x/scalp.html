    How the guard works and what it cannot do

    The guard watches the oracle price in this window and presses the page’s own Close button; Jupiter builds the close and your wallet signs it, so a wallet prompt may still need your approval. Closes fill at the oracle price about two seconds later with no price limit, and not before the 15 second minimum hold. It stops when this window closes.
